Why this exists

The stock Creality Space Pi tops out at 70 °C by firmware and throws an E4 error near its ceiling. PA/Nylon needs 80 °C and PC needs 85 °C — neither is achievable stock. Rather than sensor-offset hacks that make the display lie, we replaced the entire control side with an ESP32, an SSR-40DA, and a precision SHT40 sensor. The result is a true, displayed, tunable temperature up to 85 °C with three independent safety layers.

Safety architecture

01
Firmware limits
92 °C hard ceiling (E2), thermal runaway after 4 min no rise (E3), sensor-loss fault (E1). Fault reset gated by confirmed cool-down below 50 °C.
02
2A inline fuse
In the heater Live wire. Rated for overcurrent — independent of all software. The one component that works even if the ESP32 is dead.
03
133 °C thermal fuse
SF133E clamped to the heater body. Trips on physics alone if the SSR welds shut. Installed with copper crimps and 200 °C silicone sleeve — never soldered.
